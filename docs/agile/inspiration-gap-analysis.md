# Inspiration Gap Analysis — Atlas South vs ABM (abm.co.uk)

Captured 2026-07-29. Source: live site review of atlassouthes.com and abm.co.uk.

## Scale & Positioning
- ABM: global enterprise FM, corporate/strategic tone, industry verticals as
  primary nav (Airlines, Airports, Data Centres, Semiconductor, Government...)
- Atlas South: local London/SE trades company, urgency-driven consumer tone,
  single long-scroll homepage

## Structural Gaps Identified
1. **No dedicated service pages** — Atlas South's 8 services are homepage
   sections only, no depth (no per-service process, certifications detail,
   case studies, FAQs)
2. **No dedicated industry pages** — "Who We Serve" is a homepage grid only;
   ABM gives each industry its own page with tailored messaging
3. **No case studies / proof depth** — only 3 short reviews, no before/after,
   no client logos, no project galleries
4. **Single-page architecture** — everything lives on index.html; no
   information architecture / site nav beyond anchor links
5. **Thin commercial credibility signals** — no team/leadership content, no
   compliance/accreditation detail pages, no downloadable brochure equivalent
   to ABM's "Possibility in Action"

## What Already Works (retain)
- Instant quote forms (2x on page) — ABM has no equivalent self-serve form
- WhatsApp + phone CTAs repeated throughout — direct-response strength
- Monthly subscription tiers with Stripe/PayPal — self-serve conversion ABM lacks
- Concrete, job-level service language paired with real certifications (Gas
  Safe, Part P, SIA) — more tangible than ABM's abstracted "solutions" language
- Stats bar (700+ clients, 12k+ jobs, since 2018) — credibility marker

## Direction
Borrow: multi-page IA, per-service depth, per-industry pages, stronger proof
content (case studies, credentials-as-pages).
Keep: forms, direct-contact CTAs, pricing transparency, concrete trade language.
