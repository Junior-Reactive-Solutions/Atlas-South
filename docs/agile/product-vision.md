# Product Vision — Atlas South Website Redesign

## Vision Statement
Redesign atlassouthes.com so that Atlas South Technical Services presents with the
structural clarity, credibility, and enterprise-grade polish of a company like ABM
(abm.co.uk) — while keeping the local, fast-response, direct-conversion strengths
that already work for a London trades business (phone/WhatsApp access, instant
quote forms, subscription pricing).

ABM is the **inspiration reference**, not the target audience model — we are not
converting Atlas South into an enterprise FM brand. We are borrowing its
information architecture, visual authority, and content depth, and re-applying
them at Atlas South's scale.

## Why Now
Current site (see [`docs/agile/inspiration-gap-analysis.md`](inspiration-gap-analysis.md))
works functionally (forms, pricing, contact) but reads as a small-business page
next to ABM's structural depth: shallow industry pages, no case studies/proof
beyond 3 reviews, no dedicated per-service pages, thin content hierarchy on
scroll-heavy single page.

## Target Outcomes
- Stronger trust signals for commercial/corporate clients (not just residential)
- Clear, navigable structure per service and per industry (not one long scroll)
- Retain and strengthen what already converts: quote forms, WhatsApp/phone CTAs,
  monthly subscription tiers
- Visual design that reads credible at both residential and enterprise-facing tiers

## Out of Scope (for now)
- Backend/CRM integration beyond current form submission
- Multi-language support
- Full custom CMS build (decide platform in Sprint 0/1)

## Success Measures (draft — revisit in Sprint 0)
- Qualitative: stakeholder sign-off that new site "reads as credible as ABM" for
  commercial visitors
- Quantitative (post-launch): quote-form conversion rate, bounce rate on
  service/industry pages, time-to-quote-response unaffected by redesign
